# WordPot Commercialization & Owner Monetization Report

WordPot is uniquely positioned within the Celo and MiniPay ecosystems. This report details how the game generates revenue for you (the owner), adds more monetization loops, and describes how to implement them.

---

## 1. How You (The Owner) Earn Revenue

As the platform operator, you have multiple direct streams to monetize both competitive game lobbies and single-player modes:

```
[Staked Lobbies]      ───> 10% Platform Cut (Treasury Wallet)
[Sponsored Rooms]     ───> B2B Native Advertising Fees (Sponsors pay to customize source words)
[Daily Retries]       ───> Paid Micro-Tickets (e.g., $0.05 cUSD to retry Daily Challenge)
[Custom Creator Pots] ───> 2% Admin Hosting Service Fee
[Leaderboard Ladder]  ───> Season Ticket Sales & Point Boosters
```

### A. The Platform Rake (Direct Onchain Fee)
* **How it works**: The smart contract (`WordPotArena.sol`) has a built-in `treasuryFeeBps` (set to 10% by default). Every time a multiplayer lobby concludes:
  1. The contract aggregates the total entry stakes (the Pot).
  2. The contract calculates 10% of that total.
  3. The contract automatically transfers this 10% directly to your **treasury wallet address** during settlement.
  4. The remaining 90% is distributed to the players based on their scores.
* **Math Example**: If 5 players join a room with a 2 CELO entry fee:
  * Total Pot = 10 CELO
  * **Your Earnings** = 1 CELO (sent instantly to your treasury wallet)
  * Player Payout Pool = 9 CELO

### B. Branded Source Words & Lobbies (B2B Sponsorships)
* **How it works**: Partner with other web3 projects, tokens, or local merchants. They pay you a fixed marketing fee (e.g., $100 cUSD / week) to host sponsored rooms or themed days.
* **In-Game Flow**: During the sponsored window, all source words generated by the backend are customized to promote the sponsor (e.g., `CELO`, `VALORA`, `STABLECOIN`, `MINIPAY`). Clicking the source word details opens a promotional banner redirecting to the partner's site.

### C. Daily Challenge Micro-Tickets (Pay-to-Retry)
* **How it works**: By default, players get 1 free attempt per day to win the Daily Challenge reward. If they fail to hit the target score, instead of waiting 24 hours, they can purchase a **"Micro-Ticket"** (e.g. 0.05 cUSD) to get another attempt.
* **In-Game Flow**: Payment is processed instantly using MiniPay's low-gas transfer, routing the micro-payment directly to your treasury wallet.

### D. Season Leaderboards, Ticket Sales & Boosters (Retention Monetization)
While WordPot currently has a basic score leaderboard, you can transform it into a major revenue source:
* **Premium Season Tickets**: Run weekly or monthly "Competitive Seasons." To enter the seasonal leaderboard ladder (where top players win sponsored rewards), players buy a **Season Entry Ticket** (e.g., 0.5 CELO). 20% of ticket sales go to the owner as admin fees; 80% goes to the season prize pool.
* **Leaderboard Point Boosters**: Introduce in-game consumable items. For example, a player pays 0.1 CELO to buy a "Double Point Booster" that doubles the leaderboard points they earn for their next 3 games. This creates high-margin virtual goods sales.

## 2. Technical Updates to Support Monetization

Here is how we can implement these additional monetization pathways:

### Update A: Dynamic Treasury Fee Controls
Ensure you can adjust the platform cut depending on marketing campaigns.
* **Implementation**: We can expand the owner-only functions in `WordPotArena.sol` to allow you to toggle fees on the fly (e.g., 5% during promotional weekends to attract players, and 12% during peak tournament seasons).

### Update B: Sponsored Word Pools
* **Implementation**: Modify `server/src/rounds.js` to accept a `campaignId` query. If active, the backend pulls target words from a specific database or configuration array loaded with sponsored keywords instead of the general Datamuse pool.

### Update C: Daily Challenge Retries Endpoint
* **Implementation**:
  * Add a `/api/daily/retry-purchase` endpoint that verifies a wallet has sent a small entry transaction to your treasury address.
  * Once verified, clear the wallet's daily cooldown in `dailyPlays.json` for that specific day, letting them launch a fresh session immediately.

### Update D: Seasonal Leaderboard Reset & Booster Tracker
* **Implementation**:
  * Add a `/api/leaderboard/season-opt-in` endpoint that checks for an onchain ticket payment before registering a player into the active season database.
  * Implement a `activeBoosters` mapping in `server/src/index.js` which decreases by 1 every time a player submits a score, multiplying the leaderboard update points by $2\times$ while active.
